# MIKAY — THE DAY BEFORE — sound design

No background music anywhere (removed on request). Narration plus everyday sound
effects only, generated by `tools/make_sfx.py` and placed by `tools/build_audio.py`.

| Time | Picture | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | title card | *(silence — one clean beat before the day starts)* |
| 0:01.5 | waking up | twin-bell alarm, birdsong through the window |
| 0:05 | alarm clock insert | snooze: cloth rustle, ringing continues muffled, blanket thrown back |
| 0:09 | bathroom mirror | tap running, toothbrush, tap off and draining |
| 0:13 | the street | sneakers on concrete, birds, a lawn sprinkler, a dog behind a fence |
| 0:20 | afternoon television | a news murmur, cereal spoon, bowl set down, channel change |
| 0:26 | porch at sunset | evening birds, she stands and stretches, boots on the porch boards |
| 0:32 | night bedroom | crickets, the house settling, a notification on the nightstand |
| 0:36 | "waiting for a friend" | a low submarine swell under the last line |

**Unanswered question:** her phone buzzes twice during the television beat, and she
ignores it both times. That is the premonition — it pays off in DAY 1.
