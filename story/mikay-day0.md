# MIKAY — THE DAY BEFORE (short)

**Length** 39 s · **Format** 16:9 master, 9:16 re-crop for Shorts
**Narration** first person, past tense, calm. No dialogue, no music.

> The last ordinary day, told flat and warm, with one wrong detail at the end.

## Script

| # | Line | Picture |
| - | ---- | ------- |
| d1 | "This is what a normal day looked like. I didn't know it yet." | Mikay stretching in bed, morning sun |
| d2 | "My alarm went off at seven. I hit snooze twice, like always." | insert: the twin-bell alarm ringing |
| d3 | "I brushed my teeth in the same mirror, with the same face." | bathroom, sleepy, mirror behind her |
| d4 | "Walked to the bus stop past the same houses. The same cat sat on the same fence." | the street, sprinkler, cat, long shadows |
| d5 | "Afternoon cartoons. Then the news said a city name I had never heard of. I changed the channel." | sofa, cereal, television murmuring |
| d6 | "I sat on the porch until the streetlights came on. It was the best day I ever had." | sunset porch, phone, dog on the lawn |
| d7 | "That night I left my window open. Someone was standing under the streetlight. I figured they were waiting for a friend." | night bedroom, window open, one figure far down the road |

End card: **DAY 1. THEY'RE OUTSIDE.** — hands off to the outbreak film.

## Rules for this one

- Nothing supernatural happens on screen. The only wrong note is the figure under the
  streetlight at the very end, and it is small, still and ambiguous.
- Zero music. The day is carried by room tone and small sounds.
- Hard cuts, no camera moves, no transitions that draw attention.
- She stays on model: same face rig, same ponytail, same hoodie and pleated skirt — the
  costume from the DAY 1 film, so the two read as the same week.
