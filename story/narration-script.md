## Exact lines (this is the subtitle text — word for word, no rewrites)

```
N1  Day one. I did everything right.
N2  I nailed boards over the front door and told myself they would hold.
    Then I looked through the window. There were so many of them.
N3  Day two, they were still there. Day three... they still hadn't moved.
    So I got comfortable. I stopped sleeping behind the barricade.
    That was my mistake.
N4  Because they weren't trying to get in.
    They were looking at me the way you look at someone who doesn't know yet.
N5  Then one of them pressed its hand against the glass... and pointed up.
    I had been sleeping under that hatch for three days.
N6  So I did the only thing left. I opened the door. And they stepped aside.
```

## Subtitle rules

- Subtitles are generated **from the same source strings as the audio**, so they can
  never drift from what is spoken.
- Max 2 lines on screen, max ~42 characters per line, broken only at sentence or
  clause boundaries.
- Every subtitle is centered in the lower third, on a soft dark scrim so it stays
  readable over both the cold blue and warm amber halves of the frame.
- Nothing is paraphrased for the caption. If a line changes, the audio is re-rendered.
