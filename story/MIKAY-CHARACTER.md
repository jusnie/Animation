# MIKAY — character bible

Locked design for all frames. Any new frame must reuse these constants.

## Silhouette & shape language

- Same family as the reference hero: **white featureless oval head**, heavy black outline,
  thin black stick limbs. Never give her a nose, ears, or a normal human body.
- Head is slightly taller than wide (oval, not a circle) — this is what reads as "girl"
  in the silhouette without adding human anatomy.
- **Ponytail:** high, on her right side as we face her, thick black, sweeping back and
  down in one bold curve. It has a thin black band. It's the single strongest identifier
  at thumbnail size, so it must always break the head outline.
- **Eyes:** large black ovals, slightly taller than wide, sitting low on the face. Three
  short lash strokes to the outside of each eye. Brows are thin and readable — she
  emotes with her brow line, not her mouth.
- **Mouth:** small line mouth by default. Opens into a wide flat grin only when she's smug.

## Expressions (draw once, reuse)

1. **Neutral** — flat brows, small line mouth.
2. **Worried** — brows tilted up at the inner ends, mouth a small downward arc.
3. **Sweating** — worried + one blue drop on the cheek, brows pinched.
4. **Smug** — one eye squinted to a lid line, tiny side smirk.
5. **Terrified** — brows high, small pupils, mouth an open vertical oval.
6. **Determined** — brows angled down and in, mouth a firm line, eyes narrowed.

## Poses (draw once, reuse)

1. Palms flat on the door, head turned back over the shoulder.
2. Back against the door, sliding down, knees up.
3. Flashlight raised, looking up a dark stairwell.
4. Hand on the door bolt, mid-unlock, head turned to the horde.

## Voice & personality

- Narrates in first person, past tense, dry and a little embarrassed. Not quippy.
- Brave by decision, not by nature — each episode she chooses once and then has to
  live with it.
- Never screams. Her fear shows in her brow and her hands.

## Continuity rules

- The ponytail is never tied down or hidden, even in silhouette.
- The white eye dots must stay pure white — never shade the eyes.
- She is always the brightest thing in frame. Backgrounds stay one value step darker.
- The mini self-portrait easter egg (a framed photo, a phone lock screen) appears in one
  frame per episode.
