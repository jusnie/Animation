# MIKAY — DAY 1: THEY'RE OUTSIDE

Short-form zombie survival animation. Target length **75–90 seconds**, 16:9 primary,
re-crop to 9:16 for Shorts/Reels. No dialogue — captions + faces only.

---

## One-line pitch

A girl barricades herself in her house on day one of the outbreak — and slowly works out
that the horde outside isn't trying to get in. It's trying to warn her.

## Logline

Mikay survives her first three days behind a boarded-up door, feeling like the last
person alive. The zombies never break in. They only press their hands to the glass and
point — at the attic hatch she has been walking under the whole time.

---

## Cast

**MIKAY** — 14–16, wiry, stubborn. White oval head, dot eyes with short lash strokes,
high black ponytail, raspberry hoodie, dark skirt, black sneakers. Performance is
in her eyebrows and hands. Bravery is a decision she keeps re-making, not a trait.

**THE HORDE** — 30+ green heads, big empty eye sockets, flat palms on the glass.
Individually goofy. Collectively patient. They never snarl. They *wait*.

**THE THING UPSTAIRS** — never fully shown. Only sound: the hatch creaking, dust
falling, and in the last shot, two eyes in the attic window.

---

## Beat sheet

### 1. HOOK — 0:00–0:05
Extreme close-up of Mikay's eye, wide. A hand slams flat against the inside of the door.
Smash cut to black. Caption in chunky white: **DAY 1.**

### 2. SETUP — 0:05–0:20
Cold blue night. Mikay nails a plank across the front door, arms shaking. She peeks
through the little window: moonlight, silhouetted trees, and then — the horde. Dozens
of them. Flat palms on the glass. One sweating beat on her face, one blue drop.
She backs away and slides down the door.

### 3. FALSE SAFE — 0:20–0:42
Warm amber light. She eats cereal by lamplight, flashlight pointed at the door. Tally
marks on the wall: DAY 1, DAY 2, DAY 3. The scratching never stops, but nothing ever
breaks. She gets cocky — mimes a zombie, laughs at herself, starts sleeping on the
sofa instead of behind the barricade. Caption: **THEY NEVER COME IN.**

### 4. WRONGNESS — 0:42–0:58
Three quiet seeds, each one a small held shot:
- A zombie's expression isn't hunger. It's *concern*.
- Two of them **shake their heads "no"** when she reaches toward the hallway.
- One presses its palm to the glass and slowly **points up.**

Mikay looks up at the ceiling for the first time in three days. Caption: **WHAT ARE
THEY POINTING AT?**

### 5. TURN — 0:58–1:12
She climbs the stairs holding the flashlight. The attic hatch is open. Dust drifts
down, slowly. On the floor: muddy footprints that start at the hatch and end…
behind her. The scratching outside stops dead — all of them silent at once, all
staring up. Beat. Caption: **NOTHING SHOULD BE THAT QUIET.**

### 6. BUTTON — 1:12–1:25
Mikay runs back down and does the unthinkable: she **unbolts the front door.** It swings
open. The horde doesn't lunge — it *steps aside* to let her through, then closes ranks
behind her. Cut back to the house: dark, quiet, empty. Then the attic window: two eyes
open. Fade. Caption: **DAY 4.**
