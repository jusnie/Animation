# Style notes — reference analysis

Source: three YouTube thumbnails from a "stick figure vs. zombies" survival-story
animation channel. Filenames kept as `ref-0N_*.jpg`.

| File | Scene | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 1. Protagonist ("the stick guy")

- **Head:** white, featureless oval, heavy black outline. No ears, no hair, no nose.
- **Face:** only ever dots + mouth. Expressions are the whole performance:
  - happy: `^ ‿ ^` closed curved eyes, wide open smile
  - worried: angled brows, small oval eyes, gritted/flat mouth, one sweat drop
  - smug: one big oval eye, one small curved line, tiny smirk
- **Body:** thin black stick limbs, floating/unconnected — arms and legs attach loosely
  and overlap the torso. Deliberately crude, never rigged-looking.
- **Outfit changes every video.** The face is the only constant.

| Reference | Outfit |
| --- | --- |
| ref-01 | olive-green parka with square chest pockets + black ribbed beanie; hands behind head |
| ref-02 | blue hoodie with white drawstrings; black legs |
| ref-03 | plain red sleeveless top; black legs |

Rule: same face rig, new costume = new "season" of the same character.

## 2. Zombies

- Green skin, big empty white eye sockets with small dark pupils, dark grins with 1–2
  visible teeth, no detail inside the mouth.
- Ragged, mismatched, desaturated clothing (olive coats, teal tattered dress, grey rags).
- Darks built with **visible hatching** lines, not gradients — marker/sketch feel.
- Fingers are simple rounded stubs; hands pressed flat to glass read as a horde.
- Never scary-realistic: ugly-cute, goofy, slightly sympathetic.

## 3. Composition templates

1. **Split-screen zigzag** — hard white zigzag seam down the middle; cold blue on the
   left, warm amber on the right; one caption word per side. Sells the *premise*.
2. **Single-event tension** — character pinned against a door/wall; the threat framed in
   a window inside the frame (frame-within-frame); warm light on the near side, moonlit
   night visible through the glass; one sweat drop. Sells the *cliffhanger*.
3. **Clean emotional state** — character isolated with lots of negative space, strong
   spotlight vignette, one giant single word. Sells *resolution / relief*.

## 4. Typography

- Chunky all-caps display sans, black stroke + soft drop shadow.
  Closest free matches: Poppins ExtraBold, Nunito Black, Baloo 2 ExtraBold.
- Title palette is only three colors: **white**, **yellow**, **red**.
- Series badge = rounded **red** pill, top-left, small white caps ("DAY 1", "FULL STORY").
  The counter lives in the badge; the headline stays one or two words.
- Titles sit *in* the empty space of the frame, never overlapping the hero's face.

## 5. Recurring motif: the portal

Every reference tells the story through an opening — a window in a barricaded door, the
door itself, a phone screen on the ground. Threat is outside a surface; the hero is
inside it or just freed from it. Keep this grammar in every thumbnail and beat.

## 6. Color & light

Two palettes, never mixed:

- **Night / outside:** navy `#141B3C`, slate blue wall `#3A4A78`, cool moonlight
  `#9FB6E0`, dark navy hatch `#0B1028`
- **Interior / safe:** brick amber `#C8925A`, warm lamp yellow `#F6D67A`, wood brown
  `#6B4526`, olive `#6E7A5A`
- Accents: title yellow `#F5D31C`, badge red `#E03127`, zombie green `#7E9B5A`

Lighting = one warm source + vignette. Every surface carries a rough, chalky marker
texture; fills are imperfect and slightly translucent over the linework.

## 7. Line work

Thick black outline (roughly 6–10 px at 1280 wide), slightly wobbly, open corners.
Interior detail lines are thinner and sketchy. Nothing is a perfect geometric vector.

## 8. Easter egg

ref-01 and ref-03 both place a mini portrait of the hero inside the scene as a prop
(the phone lock screen in ref-03). Worth keeping as a recurring signature.

---

## Do / don't

**Do** keep one face rig; give him a new outfit per arc.
**Do** stage threats behind glass, doors, or screens.
**Do** use flat chalky fills over hand-drawn outlines.
**Do** caption with 1–2 all-caps words plus a small red pill badge.

**Don't** copy ref-02's rendering — glossy shaded hair, near-black clean vector lines and
semi-realistic zombie faces belong to a different family.
**Don't** add gradients, rim lights, or 3D shading to the characters.
**Don't** put more than one text block on a frame.
